Do You Support STEM Technocracy or Are Critical of It?

This quiz is set against the backdrop of a debate about the role of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) fields versus the humanities in addressing societal issues.

Neoliberal ideologies often emphasize the primacy of STEM education and technological solutions, sometimes at the expense of critical thinking and nuanced understanding provided by the humanities. This approach can lead to a depoliticized discourse on complex problems like climate change, focusing solely on technological fixes without considering the underlying systemic and societal factors.

Moreover, the quiz touches on concerns about cultural and technological imperialism, where Western technological visions and solutions are imposed on the Global South, often with detrimental consequences. Initiatives like "One Laptop per Child" and the promotion of genetically modified seeds by corporations such as Monsanto have been criticized for their top-down approach, disregard for local contexts, and potential to exacerbate existing inequalities.

The quiz aims to gauge respondents' attitudes towards these issues, categorizing them along a spectrum from STEM technocrats, who prioritize technological and data-driven solutions, to the critics, who emphasize the importance of interdisciplinary approaches, social justice, and critical theory in addressing global challenges.
