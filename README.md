# STEM Technocracy VS Critical Test

This quiz is set against the backdrop of a debate about the role of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) fields versus the humanities in addressing societal issues.

The quiz aims to gauge respondents' attitudes towards these issues, categorizing them along a spectrum from STEM technocrats, who prioritize technological and data-driven solutions, to the critics, who emphasize the importance of interdisciplinary approaches, social justice, and critical theory in addressing global challenges.

Features togglable headers and paragraphs to see the other perspective on the writing, since no writing is unbiased.
